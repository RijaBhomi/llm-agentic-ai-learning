# 📖 Curated AI & LLM Resources

## 🔬 Core Research Papers and Blogs
The absolute foundational architectures that every AI engineer should read:

* **[Attention Is All You Need (2017)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762)** – The birth of the Transformer architecture.
* **[Why Attention Is All You Need?](https://www.kaggle.com/discussions/general/493003)** – simple explainations with analogies

## 💻 Elite Video Courses & Tutorials
Deep-dive video series that map out code implementations from scratch:
* **[Cooper Codes: OpenAI Embedding Explained in 5 minutes](https://youtu.be/8kJStTRuMcs?si=VhSnGiU2Pk1DPZLk)** – the basics of OpenAI Embeddings, and how they are relevant to creating software systems that utilize custom information through embeddings.
* **[Andrej Karpathy: Let's build GPT: from scratch, in code, spelled out](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kCc8FmEb1nY)** – building a Generatively Pretrained Transformer (GPT), following the paper "Attention is All You Need" and OpenAI's GPT-2 / GPT-3.
* **[Transformers, the tech behind LLMs](https://youtu.be/wjZofJX0v4M?si=OeHyNixqGwiz3sW-)** – Breaking down how Large Language Models work, visualizing how data flows through.
* **[3Blue1Brown: Neural Networks & Transformers](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi)** – The best visual and geometric intuitions for neural networks and attention mechanics.
* **[Andrej Karpathy: Intro to Large Language Models](https://youtu.be/zjkBMFhNj_g?si=290VN7X13hoMyBRL)** – the core technical component behind systems like ChatGPT, Claude, and Bard.
